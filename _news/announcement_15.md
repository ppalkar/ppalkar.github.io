Conducted a 7-day **Workshop on Foundations of Optimization and Simulation** for about 80 officers and young professionals from the *Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS)* at IIT Delhi, May 11–21, 2026, jointly with <a href="https://kartikeyrinwa.github.io/">Prof. Kartikey Sharma</a>, <a href="https://web.iitd.ac.in/~asrvstv">Prof. Amber Srivastava</a>, <a href="https://web.iitd.ac.in/~varunr">Prof. Varun Ramamohan</a> and <a href="https://prameshk.github.io/">Prof. Pramesh Kumar</a> (Civil Engineering, IITD). The course covered Linear and Nonlinear Optimization, Integer Programming, Probability and Statistics, and Discrete Event Systems Simulation in detail, with regular theoretical and computational exercises after each topic. Participants and instructors alike thoroughly enjoyed the interactions during the course :smile:

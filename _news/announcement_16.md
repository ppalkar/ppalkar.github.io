Conducted a 5-day **Faculty Development Program on Python Programming** for 50 school teachers from Punjab at IIT Delhi, July 6–10, 2026, jointly with <a href="https://web.iitd.ac.in/~apn/index.html">Prof. Arpan Gupta</a> and <a href="https://kartikeyrinwa.github.io/">Prof. Kartikey Sharma</a>. It was a touching and enjoyable experience for the instructors and participants alike. Looking forward to contributing to the educational ecosystem at the grassroots level more often :smile:

Happy to share that our project titled "An Optimization and AI-Driven Indigenous Decision-Support Platform for Preoperative Planning of High-Impact Orthopedic Surgeries" has been recommended for funding under the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) Advanced Research Grant (ARG) program :smile: The project closely involves a team of Orthopaedic experts from the MAMC hospital (<a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DUK522wAAAAJ&hl=en">Dr. Lalit Maini</a> and Dr. Amit Tyagi) and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/meenarli-sharma-270b6421b/">Dr. Meenarli Sharma</a> as Honorary Investigators.

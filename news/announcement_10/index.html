Visit to IIT Jodhpur

Created in December 03, 2025

2025

I had a wonderful visit to IIT Jodhpur to attend the 5th International Conference on Nonlinear Applied Analysis and Optimization (ICNAAO) from December 4-6, 2025. Congratulations to the organizers on a very well-managed conference.

Snapshots of my talk.

I gave a talk on “3D Packing Optimization using Clustering and Integer Programming”. I also met a few respected professors (Prof. K.S. Mallikarjuna Rao, Prof. Vivek Borkar, Prof. S. K. Neogy) and friends (Prof. Parag Arvind Deshpande, Prof. Jai Narayan Tripathi, Prof. Dinesh Mohan Joshi, and made new ones too)

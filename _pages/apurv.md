I am an Assistant Professor in the Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (IEOR) group in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at IIT Delhi (since December 2022). I work on theory, algorithms, software, and applications of Optimization.

In particular, I work a lot on problems from Indian Railways (timetabling, dynamic pricing, etc.) using Mixed-Integer Linear Optimization, Constraint Programming, and AI/ML techniques.
